# 记录 / Capturing Notes

记录是 Elsewhen 的核心：**随手记一句，AI 稍后自动理解它**。Recording is the core of Elsewhen: **jot a line, and AI understands it later.**

## 快速记录 / Quick Capture

一个小浮动窗口，适合「随手记一句」。A small floating window for one-liners.

- 按快捷键唤出（默认 `Super+Space`，即 Win/Command+空格）。
- 窗口会一直在最前、光标自动落在输入框。
- **回车**提交，**Esc**关闭。
- 提交后立刻完成，不用等 AI。

- Open it with the shortcut (default `Super+Space`).
- The window stays on top with the cursor ready.
- **Enter** submits, **Esc** closes.
- It saves instantly — no waiting for AI.

## 在对话里记录 / Recording in a Conversation

主界面底部的输入框也能记。输入一句经历，发送即可——它既是对话，也会被记为一条记录。

The main input box records too. Send a sentence — it becomes both a chat message and a recorded note.

## 记录还是闲聊？/ Record or Just Chat?

每条消息会被 AI 标成「记录」或「讨论」：

- **记录**：正经的经历、想法、决定，会进入你的日流和长期记忆。
- **讨论**：闲聊、无关紧要的话，不会混进你的记录。
- **分析中**：AI 还在判断。

在消息旁的小标签上点一下，可以手动改成「纳入记录」「作为讨论」，或让 AI「重新分析」。改错了也没关系，原话不会丢，只是调整它算不算记录。

Each message is tagged **记录** (record) or **讨论** (chat):

- **记录**: real experiences, thoughts, decisions — goes into your daily stream and long-term memory.
- **讨论**: chit-chat — doesn't mix into your records.
- **分析中**: AI is still deciding.

Tap the small tag next to a message to change it to 纳入记录 (record), 作为讨论 (chat), or 重新分析 (re-analyze). You can always correct it later — the original text is never lost.

## AI 什么时候处理？/ When Does AI Process It?

你记下之后，AI 会在**后台自动**处理（理解内容、提取人物/项目/主题）。你不用做任何事，也不需要等它。可以在 **设置 → 数据 → 事件分析队列** 看到处理进度。

After you record, AI processes it **in the background automatically** (understanding the content, extracting people/projects/topics). You don't need to do anything or wait. Progress is visible under **Settings → 数据 → 事件分析队列**.
